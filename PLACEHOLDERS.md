## Replacing a hero photo

Hero photos are full-bleed and cropped with `object-cover`, so they need to be
**landscape and at least 2000px wide**. The subject should sit in the middle
horizontal band — the top and bottom get cropped at wide viewports — and the
left third should stay visually calm, since the headline, intro, CTA and trust
badges are overlaid there on a dark scrim.
